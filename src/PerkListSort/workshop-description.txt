[b]Sorts the perk and talent pickers alphabetically.[/b]

Vanilla's order is neither alphabetical nor unlock order -- it is the game's internal class table, reversed. Finding one perk means reading every row.

It only reorders. Nothing is added, removed or rebalanced, and nothing of the mod's goes into your save, so it is safe to add or remove mid-campaign.
