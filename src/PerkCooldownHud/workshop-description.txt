[b]See how long until a triggered perk is ready again, instead of guessing.[/b]

Blurred Silhouette just fired and saved you. It will do it again in a few turns -- but how many? Vanilla deletes the icon the moment the active phase ends, so there is nothing left on screen to read. You either keep a count in your head or you push your luck and find out.

I got tired of finding out. So this mod keeps the icon up through the cooldown and puts the number right on it.

[h2]The legend[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]No icon[/b] -- ready. Go.
[*][b]Green border[/b] -- active right now. That is vanilla, untouched.
[*][b]Yellow border, dimmed, with a number[/b] -- cooling down, and that is how much longer.
[/list]

When it vanishes again, you are back in business.

[h2]Why yellow, and why the border[/h2]

Green and red already mean buff and debuff everywhere else in the mission HUD. A third colour reads instantly. A dimmed green would have to be compared against an undimmed one before it told you anything, and you do not want to be doing that with a Quasimorph breathing on you.

The yellow is not something I made up either. The panel already ships that exact sprite for mouse-over, so it sits inside the game's palette instead of shouting next to it.

[h2]A few things worth knowing[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]That number is the game's own countdown[/b], not something reconstructed by counting turns. It is the same value your character sheet calls cooldown remaining, so it cannot drift out of sync with the real thing.
[*][b]It sometimes drops by more than one per turn.[/b] Not a glitch -- perks with cooldown recovery really do tick down faster, and you are watching that happen.
[*][b]A number in the hundreds is also not a glitch.[/b] A few perks measure their cooldown in action points rather than turns. Those start high and fall quickly.
[*][b]No new noise.[/b] The vanilla panel flashes white and plays a sound every time its number changes. Left alone, that would ping you once per turn for the whole cooldown. It is muted for the counter and only the counter.
[*][b]It only draws.[/b] No timings changed, no balance changed, nothing about how perks actually behave. Just a thing you can see now that you could not see before.
[/list]

[h2]Getting it[/h2]

Subscribe, and that is the whole install. It uses the game's own mod loader, so there is no BepInEx, no config file, nothing to set up and nothing to read first.

Add it or drop it mid-campaign as you like. Unsubscribing puts you straight back to stock, because none of this mod ever goes into your save.
