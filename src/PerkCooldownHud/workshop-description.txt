[b]Shows how many turns until a triggered perk is ready again.[/b]

Vanilla draws a perk's icon only while the perk is active. The moment the active phase
ends the icon disappears, and nothing tells you how long until Blurred Silhouette can save
you a second time. This mod keeps the icon up through the cooldown, dimmed, with the turns
remaining on it.

[table]
[tr][th]Perk state[/th][th]Vanilla[/th][th]With this mod[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Ready[/td][td]no icon[/td][td]no icon[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Active[/td][td]icon, green border[/td][td]unchanged[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Cooling down[/td][td][b]no icon at all[/b][/td][td]yellow border, dimmed, counting down[/td][/tr]
[/table]

No icon means ready, green means active, yellow means cooling.

[b]What it does not change[/b]

It only draws. No timings, no balance, nothing about how perks behave, and nothing of it
goes into your save -- add it or drop it mid-campaign.

[b]Worth knowing[/b]

[list]
[*][b]The number is the game's own countdown[/b], the same one your character sheet shows.
It cannot drift out of sync.
[*][b]It can drop by more than one per turn.[/b] Perks with cooldown recovery really do
tick down faster.
[*][b]A number in the hundreds is not a glitch.[/b] A few perks count their cooldown in
action points rather than turns.
[/list]
